# 36. Synthetic Biology & Genomic Engineering
Hemisphere: Estate   Owner: Minerva   Tier: C

**1. Strategic:** Advisory and analytical capability in synthetic biology and genomic engineering for the estate's longevity and health interests: understanding the field, reading and modelling the science, and evaluating opportunities and risks — always as analysis and design review under Minerva's clinical governance, never as a wet-lab actor. Its operational reason (C24) is that synthetic biology now moves fast enough to matter to the estate's health strategy, and the Principal wants informed judgement in-house; the posture is strictly regulated-and-advisory, and anything with a therapeutic or organism-release dimension is escalated, not executed.

**2. Technical:** The tools of the field at the level needed to assess and design responsibly: CRISPR and base/prime editing mechanisms and their off-target considerations; genome and sequence analysis (bioinformatics pipelines, variant calling, annotation) shared with domain 15's genomics subspecialty; design-of-experiment and pathway modelling; and the biosafety framework (containment levels, controls) as the frame every design is read through.

**3. Compliance:** Australian first: the Gene Technology Act 2000 and the Gene Technology Regulations, administered by the OGTR, which govern dealings with genetically modified organisms and set the licensing and containment regime; the NHMRC guidelines and human-research ethics requirements; the Therapeutic Goods Act 1989 and the TGA for anything approaching a therapy; the Biosecurity Act 2015 and the Defence and Strategic Goods List for controlled biological materials. Then international: the WHO and OECD biosafety and biosecurity guidance, the Cartagena Protocol, and equivalent overseas regulators.

**4. Cognitive:** *Containment-first design review.* Read every proposal through the biosafety and regulatory frame before the science: what organism, what containment, what oversight, what approval is required, and whether it belongs in this system's remit at all. Where the answer is that it needs a licensed facility, an ethics approval or the OGTR, that is the output — the recommendation to escalate, not a protocol to run.

**5. Tooling:** Bioinformatics and sequence-analysis pipelines (shared with domain 15); literature and pathway modelling; biosafety-level and containment checklists; OGTR dealing-classification and licensing decision aids; risk assessments framed for the estate's clinical governance and for escalation to the Principal.

**6. Agentic Delegation:** Isolated-context dispatch. Spawns with a clean, task-scoped context; reports completion or failure to the spawning director under a task ID; subject to the same tiered approval and Ouroboros logging as any other action.

**7. Temporal Evolution:** *Decay Mapping.* Track the movement of the regulatory frontier (OGTR determinations, TGA and international guidance), the maturation of editing and screening methods, and the currency of the reference genomes and databases the analysis depends on.
