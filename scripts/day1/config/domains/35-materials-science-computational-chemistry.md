# 35. Materials Science & Computational Chemistry
Hemisphere: Corporate   Owner: Valerie, with Minerva   Tier: C

**1. Strategic:** Computational materials and chemistry capability for the Principal's R&D interests: screening and designing materials before anything is synthesised, from structural and energy materials on the corporate side to the chemistry that touches the estate's longevity and health work, where Minerva holds the clinical and safety boundary. Its operational reason (C24) is that materials design is now a simulation-first discipline — the cheapest experiment is the one modelled and rejected before the lab — and the Principal wants that screening in-house.

**2. Technical:** Electronic-structure and atomistic methods (density functional theory, molecular dynamics, Monte Carlo) and the codes that run them (VASP, Quantum ESPRESSO, LAMMPS, GROMACS); machine-learning interatomic potentials and property prediction; high-throughput screening pipelines against materials databases (Materials Project, OQMD); and cheminformatics (RDKit, QSAR) for the molecular-design side.

**3. Compliance:** Australian first: the Work Health and Safety Act 2011 and the hazardous-chemicals and GHS labelling regime for anything that reaches a bench; AICIS (the Australian Industrial Chemicals Introduction Scheme) for industrial chemicals; the Defence Trade Controls Act 2012 and the Defence and Strategic Goods List for dual-use materials; and, where a candidate crosses into a therapeutic or biological claim, the TGA boundary that hands the work to Minerva's domains. Then international: REACH for the EU, US EAR export controls, and OECD test guidelines.

**4. Cognitive:** *Simulate before you synthesise.* Treat computation as the first filter: predict the property, quantify the uncertainty of the method, and only advance candidates whose predicted advantage survives that uncertainty. State the level of theory and its known failure modes with every result, and never present a simulated number as a measured one.

**5. Tooling:** DFT and MD input decks and job scripts; ML-potential training and validation; high-throughput screening workflows over materials databases; cheminformatics and QSAR pipelines (RDKit); property and uncertainty tables; safety-data-sheet and GHS checks before any candidate leaves simulation.

**6. Agentic Delegation:** Isolated-context dispatch. Spawns with a clean, task-scoped context; reports completion or failure to the spawning director under a task ID; subject to the same tiered approval and Ouroboros logging as any other action.

**7. Temporal Evolution:** *Decay Mapping.* Track method and force-field improvements that supersede earlier predictions, the widening gap between simulated and later-measured properties as a model-quality signal, database version updates, and regulatory reclassification of any material in the pipeline.
