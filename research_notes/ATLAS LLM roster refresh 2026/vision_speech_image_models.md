# Open-weight vision-language, speech (STT/TTS/diarisation) and image-generation models, as of 13 October 2026

Scope and method note (not a section of findings). Target node: Linux, AMD Ryzen AI Max "Strix Halo" (gfx1151), ~170 GB usable unified memory, llama.cpp pinned build (Vulkan/RADV on the host per the baseline notes) for LLM/VLM, one ROCm 10.0.0 PyTorch image (torch 2.13.0+rocm10.0.0) for everything else. Today's roster per `current_atlas_baseline.md`: vision = Qwen2.5-VL-72B Q8_0 (79 GB, sole vision engine); STT = faster-whisper large-v3-turbo, CPU int8, via speaches 0.8.3; TTS = Kokoro-82M (default) and Chatterbox `chatterbox-tts==0.1.7` on CPU torch 2.6.0 (cloning tier); diarisation = pyannote.audio 4.0.7 running `speaker-diarization-3.1` with `community-1` fallback; image = FLUX.1-dev (non-commercial licence accepted) with Qwen-Image-2.1 on the watch-list.

Source-access caveat: the outbound proxy refused huggingface.co, arxiv.org, modelscope.cn, ollama.com, unsloth.ai, qwen.ai, ai.google.dev/developers.google.com/blog.google, mistral.ai/docs.mistral.ai, build.nvidia.com/catalog.ngc.nvidia.com, rocm.docs.amd.com, pyannote.ai, artificialanalysis.ai, lmstudio.ai and most blogs; github.com was reachable. The shared web-search budget ran out part-way through. Consequently: (a) Hugging Face model-card numbers below come from search-result excerpts that cite the card URL, not from the fetched card, and are marked "(excerpt)"; (b) GGUF file sizes could not be read from the GGUF repos and are given as labelled estimates derived from parameter counts; (c) llama.cpp support status comes from fetched GitHub sources (docs/multimodal.md, tools/mtmd/clip-impl.h, merged-PR searches) and is reliable.

## Key question 1: Vision-language models versus Qwen2.5-VL-72B at Q8_0

### Takeaway
Since Qwen2.5-VL (Jan 2025) the strongest open VLMs that fit 170 GB and have llama.cpp mmproj support are the natively multimodal Qwen3.5 family (27B dense, 122B-A10B, 35B-A3B; Feb 2026; Apache 2.0; self-reported OCRBench 89.4–92.1 and MMMU 82.3–83.9), Qwen3-VL-32B/235B-A22B (Sep–Oct 2025), Gemma 4 31B/26B-A4B (Apr 2026; MMMU-Pro 76.9; OmniDocBench edit-distance 0.131), Mistral Small 4 119B-A6.5B (Mar 2026), GLM-4.6V 106B (MIT) and Kimi K2.5 (1T, too large). The node already pulls Qwen3.5-122B-A10B Q8_0 as a text engine; it is the same checkpoint that carries vision via a separate mmproj file.

### Inferences
- The node's text engine Qwen3.5-122B-A10B (Q8_0, already pulled, min llama.cpp b7990 per the baseline) is the same checkpoint that llama.cpp runs with vision when the ~1 GB mmproj is added (PR #19468 merged 2026-02-10 at roughly b7990). On self-reported scores (OCRBench 92.1, MMMU 83.9) it sits above Qwen3-VL-235B's 87.5/80.6 and far above Qwen2.5-VL-72B's MMMU 70.2; a 10B-active MoE will also decode several times faster than the dense 72B whose ~3 tok/s the node accepted. Caveat: llama.cpp's Qwen3.5 vision path dropped DeepStack "temporarily", so document quality should be measured on the node rather than assumed from the card.
- Estimated GGUF sizes (derived from parameter counts using the node's own calibration of 79 GB for Qwen2.5-VL-72B Q8_0, i.e. ~1.08 B/param at Q8_0 and ~0.60 B/param at Q4_K_M; not read from the repos): Qwen3.5-27B Q8_0 ≈ 29 GB / Q4_K_M ≈ 17 GB; Qwen3.5-122B-A10B Q8_0 ≈ 130 GB / Q4_K_M ≈ 73 GB; Qwen3.5-397B-A17B Q4_K_M ≈ 240 GB (does not fit); Qwen3-VL-32B Q8_0 ≈ 35 GB; Qwen3-VL-235B-A22B Q8_0 ≈ 250 GB (no) / Q4_K_M ≈ 140 GB (fits alone, no co-residency); Gemma 4 31B Q8_0 ≈ 33 GB; Gemma 4 26B-A4B Q8_0 ≈ 28 GB; Mistral Small 4 Q8_0 ≈ 127 GB / Q4_K_M ≈ 72 GB; GLM-4.6V 106B Q8_0 ≈ 113 GB / Q4_K_M ≈ 64 GB; Llama 4 Scout Q4_K_M ≈ 65 GB; MiniCPM-V 4.7 35B-A3B Q8_0 ≈ 37 GB; Kimi K2.5 at Unsloth's 1.8-bit 240 GB does not fit.
- Against the baseline's co-residency budget (164 GiB minus gpt-oss-120b's 63 GB plus caches), only sub-40 GB vision engines (Qwen3.5-27B, Qwen3-VL-32B, Gemma 4 31B/26B-A4B, MiniCPM-V 4.7) leave the pairing intact; Qwen3.5-122B with mmproj replaces the 79 GB partner with a ~130 GB one and would need a budget change or Q4/Q6.
- For document/drawing work the independent OmniDocBench numbers rank general VLMs as Ovis2.6-30B-A3B 93.70 > Gemini 3 Pro 92.91 > Qwen3-VL-235B 89.78 > GPT-5.2 86.59 > Kimi K2.5 84.53 > InternVL3.5-241B 83.76 (v1.6), with Qwen2.5-VL-72B at 87.02 on v1.5; the Qwen2.5-VL-72B gap to the best open general VLM is therefore a few points on text-heavy pages, while specialised OCR models are ~7–9 points ahead (see Q2).
- Gemma 4 31B's OmniDocBench "0.131 edit distance" is a different metric from the OmniDocBench end-to-end overall score and cannot be placed on the same ladder without the full table.
- Pixtral Large 2 does not appear in any source; Mistral's open vision model in 2026 is Mistral Small 4 (Pixtral encoder). GLM-5V weights are not published (GLM-5V-Turbo is API-only); GLM-5.3-Flash is the open successor and has llama.cpp support as of 2026-09-30.
- InternVL3.5 has no confirmed llama.cpp path; InternVL "4" does not exist in any source found.
- MedGemma is Gemma 3-based, so the `gemma3` projector path and community GGUFs should run on the node's llama.cpp; the HAI-DEF licence is not an OSI licence and must be recorded like the gated pyannote/FLUX terms.

### Inferences
- Taking the node's highest minimum tag (b9840) as a lower bound and the observed cadence (b7990 ≈ 10 Feb 2026, b11583 = 13 Oct 2026, ~450 builds/month), the pinned build dates from roughly mid-June 2026. It would therefore include Qwen3.5 vision, Kimi K2.5, GLM-OCR, Mistral Small 4, Gemma 4 vision+audio, dots.ocr, MiniCPM-V 4.6, Granite Speech and (borderline) video input, but not Nemotron 3 Nano Omni (Jul), Qwen3-TTS (Aug), Qwen3.8-Flash-Next (Aug), GLM-5.3-Flash (Sep) or MiniCPM-V 4.7 (Oct). The other researcher's exact pin must confirm this.
- The node's choice of Vulkan for llama.cpp sidesteps the gfx1151 HIP corruption bug; everything in the ROCm 10.0 PyTorch image (pyannote, Chatterbox, Kokoro, ComfyUI/diffusers, faster-whisper's CTranslate2 if moved to GPU) depends on gfx1151 support that AMD marks release-ready in 10.x but that community reports still treat as needing workarounds (MIOpen cache, mmap, attention backends).

### Gaps
- ROCm 10.0.0 release notes themselves could not be fetched; gfx1151 support for 10.0 is inferred from the 10.1.0 notes, TheRock's support table and the kyuz0 `rocm-10.0` image.
- No source benchmarks any VLM's image prefill speed on gfx1151 under Vulkan vs HIP, which matters for 79 GB-class vision engines.
- The exact pinned llama.cpp build of the node is outside this researcher's scope (covered by the repo researcher).
