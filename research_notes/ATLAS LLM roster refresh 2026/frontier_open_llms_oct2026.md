## Key question 1: DeepSeek — latest open release, checkpoints, sizes; does "DeepSeek V4.1" exist?

### Takeaway
DeepSeek's current open-weight line is V4 (April 2026) with two later checkpoints, V4-Flash-0731 (284B-A13B, MIT, 31 Jul 2026) and V4-Pro-0813 (1.6T-A49B, MIT, 13 Aug 2026), a V4-Flash-Vision-Exp (31 Aug 2026), and a genuinely new open-weight model, DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash (MIT, 10 Sep 2026, ~552B backbone, 8B active on input / 16B on generation, 1M context, image input). V4.1-Flash is real and downloadable, but it does not fit 170 GB (Q2_K alone is 246 GiB) and mainline llama.cpp support is still an open PR as of 13 Oct 2026. No "V4.1-Pro" open release was found.

## Key question 2: Qwen — latest open release and size ladder; does "Qwen 3.8" exist?

### Takeaway
Qwen 3.8 exists and is the latest open family: Qwen3.8-2.4T-A95B (open weights 12 Aug 2026), Qwen3.8-27B (dense, native multimodal, Apache 2.0, 14 Aug 2026) and Qwen3.8-Flash-Next (125B main + 51B n-gram embeddings, 6B active, "early preview of the architecture used in Qwen4", 26 Aug 2026, llama.cpp-supported). Qwen3.7 never shipped open weights (Plus/Max are API-only). Qwen3.6 open models are only 35B-A3B (16 Apr 2026) and 27B (22 Apr 2026). The last open ~120B Qwen MoE therefore remains Qwen3.5-122B-A10B (24 Feb 2026), unless Qwen3.8-Flash-Next is treated as its successor.

### Takeaway
