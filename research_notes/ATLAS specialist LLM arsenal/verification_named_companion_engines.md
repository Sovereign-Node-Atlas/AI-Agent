     - EG2 tops out at 768 (MRL 512/256/128), so the 1024 schema must change.
     - Qwen3-Embedding supports "user-defined output dimensions ranging from 32 to 4096" ([card](https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3-Embedding-8B/raw/main/README.md)).
     - Nemotron-3-Embed can be sliced to 1024 with re-normalisation (card).
