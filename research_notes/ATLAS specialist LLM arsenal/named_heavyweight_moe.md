## Key question 1: Identity — latest open checkpoint, parameters, architecture, native precision, context, vision, licence

### Takeaway
Only three of the five are 2026-generation models (DeepSeek-V4-Flash-0731, GLM-5.3-Flash, Qwen3.8-Flash-Next); Qwen3-235B-A22B (last checkpoints July and September 2025) and Llama 4 Maverick (April 2025, no update since) are 2025-generation. 0731 remains the latest text checkpoint of V4-Flash; the two newer DeepSeek releases are a vision variant of the same architecture (V4-Flash-Vision-Exp, 31 Aug 2026) and a different, 552B model (V4.1-Flash, 10 Sep 2026) that does not fit. Licences: MIT (DeepSeek, GLM), Apache 2.0 (Qwen3-235B family), Llama 4 Community License (Maverick), Qwen Community License 1.0 (Flash-Next).

## Key question 2: GGUF rungs and which fit alone in 164 GiB with KV room at 32K, 64K and 128K

### Takeaway
Fitting at the least-quantised level differs sharply: V4-Flash fits at its lossless UD-Q8_K_XL (150.75 GiB) and GLM-5.3-Flash only at about 4 bits (UD-IQ4_XS 146.05 GiB, its UD-Q4_K_XL is 185.99 GiB). Qwen3-235B tops out at Q5_K_S (150.76 GiB) for short context or Q4_K_M/Q4_1 for 128K. Maverick fits only at 2-to-3-bit (UD-Q2_K_XL 142.17 GiB, UD-IQ3_XXS 157.69 GiB). Flash-Next has two near-8-bit options: unsloth UD-Q6_K_XL (157.55 GiB) and ggml-org's Q8_0 (151.46 GiB), which keeps every transformer weight at Q8_0 but stores the 51B n-gram table at Q4_0.

### Inferences

Maverick at UD-Q2_K_XL (17B active, ~3 bpw) has no anchor; its active-weight bytes (~6-7 GB/token) suggest a ceiling near 40 tok/s, realistic perhaps 20-25 (unverified inference).
- Prefill on mainline Vulkan is the weak point for V4-Flash at long context (51 tok/s at 64K means ~20 min to ingest a fresh 64K prompt), unlike Flash-Next (hundreds to >1,000 tok/s on ROCm forks).
