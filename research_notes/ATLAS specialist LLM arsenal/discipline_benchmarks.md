# Discipline-by-discipline benchmark evidence for the large candidate engines (as of 2026-10-15)

Scope and labels used throughout. Every value below was read on 2026-10-15 from the primary page or its data file unless it is marked "(10-14 notes)", which means it is reused from the primary-source evidence table in `/home/user/AI-Agent/research_notes/ATLAS LLM roster refresh 2026/gap_closure_2026-10-14.md` (read 2026-10-14), or "(search excerpt)", which means it comes only from a search-engine summary and is unverified. Evidence types: **[I]** the third party ran the model itself on its own harness (Vals AI, Artificial Analysis, LMArena human votes, EQ-Bench, MathArena, Context Arena); **[I-sub]** an independent board that lists results submitted by the model vendor or by an agent builder (official SWE-bench, tbench.ai, CyberGym); **[V]** the vendor's own model card; **[A]** an aggregator. "In-envelope" means one of the assignment's candidates (≥ ~70B total parameters, open weights, runnable alone in ~164 GiB at some GGUF rung according to the sibling notes `named_heavyweight_moe.md`, `named_120b_class_and_uncensored.md` and `quantization_value.md`); fit at a particular rung is not recomputed here. Larger open models (DeepSeek V4.1 Flash 552B, GLM-5.3 753B, Kimi K3, MiMo-V2.6-Pro, Qwen3.8 Max, Nemotron 3 Ultra, MiniMax-M3, Mistral Large 4, Hy4) appear only as "best open (any size)" references. **None of the scores below was measured on a quantised GGUF; all are hosted API or vendor runs at the provider's precision.**

Principal source mechanics (for the report writer): Vals AI pages embed the full leaderboard as an HTML table (rank, model, score, cost, latency) — [Vals benchmarks index](https://www.vals.ai/benchmarks). Artificial Analysis (AA) model pages embed a payload with 700 model records (index, component fields, six "capability" sub-scores); all AA values here come from one read of [AA GLM-5.3-Flash page](https://artificialanalysis.ai/models/glm-5-3-flash) whose HTML states "Intelligence Index v4.3.2 incorporates 10 evaluations…". LMArena values come from LMArena's own export [lmarena-ai/leaderboard-dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/lmarena-ai/leaderboard-dataset), `text/latest` parquet, `leaderboard_publish_date` 2026-10-13, 29 categories.

## Key question 1: Per-discipline leader tables — scores, benchmark versions and dates, independent runs versus vendor claims

### Takeaway
Independent, same-harness evidence now exists for most disciplines, chiefly from Vals AI (legal, finance, tax, medicine, coding, cyber, maths), Artificial Analysis v4.3.2 (reasoning, agents, long context, domain capability sub-scores, medical long-context, legal agents) and LMArena's occupational categories (legal, finance, medicine, writing, software). Among in-envelope candidates, **GLM-5.3-Flash** is the in-envelope leader on most legal, finance/tax, medical-documentation, agentic, long-context and human-preference boards; **MiMo-V2.6-Flash** leads on cyber (Vals CyberBench), Terminal-Bench 2.1 (Vals), maths proofs and the GDP-weighted Vals Index; **Qwen3.8-Flash-Next** leads on GPQA, Terminal-Bench 2.1 (AA harness) and MMMU-Pro, and is level with MiMo-V2.6-Flash on LMArena WebDev (1633.2 vs 1635.1); **DeepSeek-V4-Flash-0731** leads on LiveCodeBench (Vals), CorpFin and MedCode; the 2025-generation models (Qwen3-235B-2507, Llama 4 Maverick, gpt-oss-120b, Mistral Large 2) trail far behind on every current board. Creative writing (EQ-Bench), Australian law, HealthBench, CTIBench, Cybench, Fiction.LiveBench, OCRBench and MMLU-ProX have no independent in-envelope evidence beyond what is listed.
